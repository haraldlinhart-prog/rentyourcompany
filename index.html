At the End of the Lease

A clear handback, clearly documented

Once the agreed lease term ends, the company is handed back. The company name is changed in the relevant official register (e.g. Companies House in the UK, the state registry in the US, the Handelsregister in Germany, or the Companies Registry in Hong Kong) to clearly document the end of your activity as tenant.

Beneficial owners are disclosed wherever legally required. An extension of the lease term is always possible on request, provided this is arranged in good time before the term ends.

Key Points

  • Debt-free company — no legacy liabilities or open matters
  • Name change after handback — documents the end of your activity in the official register
  • Disclosure of beneficial owners — wherever legally required
  • Extension available — beyond the original lease term, on request
